Lisbon in four evenings

By Marta Reis · 8 min read

Fado is best heard in a small room, a glass of vinho verde in hand, a few steps from the river. On Fridays the tavernas of Alfama open their doors to anyone who wanders by.

Posters like this one cover the walls around Largo do Chafariz de Dentro. Most are in Portuguese only, so it helps to read them where they hang.

Summer is busy. If you can, come in late spring or early autumn, when the light is soft and the queues for the 28 tram are short.
