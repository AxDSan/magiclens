Features

Text you couldn't touch, now you can.

Any image, anywhere

Photos, screenshots, memes, CSS backgrounds, canvases, video frames, inline SVG, or any element you pick on the page.

Pixel-perfect text layer

Every word sits on its own glyphs, stretched to their exact width. Copy gives you clean spaces and line breaks.

Local OCR, offline

Tesseract and PaddleOCR run inside your browser. No account, no upload, nothing leaves your device.

Translate in place

The translation is painted over the original paragraphs, like a printed page in your language.

Ask the image

Explain, summarize, or ask anything. Bring your own key, or run a small vision model locally with Ollama or LM Studio.

Smooth on busy pages

Every effect runs on the compositor. The page you're reading never stutters while MagicLens works.

How it works

Three moves. No learning curve.

  1. 1

    Point

    Hover an image and click the lens, right-click it, or press Alt+Shift+L.

  2. 2

    Read

    Local OCR finds every word. With an AI provider, Hybrid mode lets the model correct it while keeping exact positions.

  3. 3

    Use it

    Select, copy, translate in place, or ask a question. Esc puts the image back the way it was.

Privacy

It collects nothing.
Really, nothing.

There is no MagicLens server. Local OCR never touches the network. If you connect an AI provider, images go straight from your browser to the provider you picked, with your own key, only when you ask.
